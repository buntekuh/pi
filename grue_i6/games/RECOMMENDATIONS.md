# Inform 6 games recommended for Grue conversion

These are open source Inform 6 games with published walkthroughs, selected as
conversion targets to validate Grue's feature coverage and ensure compiled games
play as intended. Each game comes with a source file and a walkthrough that can
be used to write the test suite.

★ = priority pick

---

## ★ Balances
**Author:** Graham Nelson  
**Size:** ~5 rooms  
**Theme:** Zork universe — magical puzzles involving weights, spells, and cubes  
**Why:** Written by Graham Nelson specifically as an Inform 6 teaching example.
Canonical, minimal, and the most readable I6 source you will find. Ships with
the Inform distribution.

- Source: https://ifarchive.org/if-archive/games/source/inform/balances.inf  
- Walkthrough: https://plover.net/~davidw/sol/b/balan94.html

---

## ★ All Things Devours
**Author:** half sick of shadows  
**Size:** Small (single complex puzzle — time travel)  
**Theme:** You must destroy your own time machine before the government finds it  
**Why:** XYZZY Award for Best Individual Puzzle (2004 IF Comp, 3rd place).
Tight, well-documented design. Official walkthrough from the author. Tests
state-machine puzzle patterns and stealth mechanics not covered by synthetic
test files.

- Source: https://ifarchive.org/if-archive/games/source/inform/devours.inf  
- Walkthrough: author's site + IFWiki

---

## ★ Escape from the Troll's Cave
**Author:** Garry Francis  
**Size:** Small  
**Theme:** Student carnival escape scenario  
**Why:** Modern I6 code (2024), actively maintained on GitHub, uses PunyInform
library. Easiest to inspect and the closest to contemporary I6 style.

- Source: https://github.com/warrigal24/escape-from-the-trolls-cave  
- Walkthrough: https://plover.net/~davidw/sol/e/escap24.html

---

## Adventureland
**Author:** Scott Adams (original); Inform 6 port by Graham Nelson  
**Size:** Medium  
**Theme:** Classic treasure hunt — the first commercial text adventure (1978)  
**Why:** Historical significance. Graham Nelson's port is a canonical example of
adapting a classic game to I6. Extensive walkthroughs everywhere.

- Source: https://ifarchive.org/if-archive/games/source/inform/Adventureland-r1-961111.inf  
- Walkthrough: https://solutionarchive.com + classic gaming sites

---

## Blue Chairs
**Author:** Chris Klimas  
**Size:** Small–medium  
**Theme:** Puzzle-centric  
**Why:** Official walkthrough and design document included in the archive.

- Source: https://ifarchive.org/if-archive/games/source/inform/bluechairs-source.zip  
- Walkthrough: included in source archive

## Buried in Shoes
**Author:** Kazuki Mishima  
**Size:** ~5–10 rooms  
**Theme:** Mystery/puzzle  
**Why:** Small scope, David Welbourn walkthrough available. Good for a quick
conversion exercise.

- Source: https://ifarchive.org/if-archive/games/source/inform/bishoes.zip  
- Walkthrough: https://plover.net/~davidw/

---

## The Dreamhold
**Author:** Andrew Plotkin  
**Size:** Small–medium (multiple chapters)  
**Theme:** Tutorial game designed to teach IF conventions to new players  
**Why:** Tutorial structure makes the expected play sequence completely explicit.
Built-in guidance doubles as a walkthrough. Note: personal/educational use only.

- Source: https://zarfhome.com/dreamhold/ + IF Archive  
- Walkthrough: built-in tutorial structure

---

## Adventure (Colossal Cave)
**Author:** Inform 6 port by Graham Nelson  
**Size:** ~40 rooms  
**Theme:** The original cave exploration game  
**Why:** Most documented interactive fiction game ever made — walkthroughs,
maps, and analyses everywhere. Too large to start with but a long-term target
for proving Grue's completeness.

- Source: IF Archive  
- Walkthrough: everywhere

---

## 2604
